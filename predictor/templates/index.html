Carat

Carat is the weight of the diamond. 1 carat = 0.2 grams. Higher carat usually means a larger diamond.

Cut

Cut refers to how well a diamond is shaped and faceted. Excellent cut maximizes brilliance and sparkle.

Color

Color is graded from D (colorless) to J (slightly colored). Colorless diamonds are more valuable.

Clarity

Clarity measures how free the diamond is from internal flaws (inclusions) and surface defects. Higher clarity means higher value.
